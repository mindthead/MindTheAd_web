Why can't we stop scrolling?

Have you ever been bored and decided to sit down and scroll on Instagram for a bit? You scroll through videos, and suddenly an hour has passed. This is scroll addiction, an endless loop you can't seem to break. Let's break down how social media affects us and why we stay stuck in this vicious cycle for so long.

First of all, the app isn't your enemy, but it is designed to keep you inside. As we scroll, our brain treats every new post as a small reward. Dopamine is released — the same chemical that activates when something exciting or unpredictable happens. This variable reward system is what social media exploits perfectly: you never know if the next scroll will show something boring or something brilliantly interesting. It's this uncertainty that keeps us hooked.

A 2023 study found that among 12–17-year-olds in the U.S., 50.4% spend more than 4 hours daily on screens, and 22.8% spend 3 hours a day. Think about how many of those four hours started with “I'll just check my friends’ posts for five minutes.”

Another factor is how much social media influences us. When we open an app and see that those we follow are on a perfect vacation, posted a great assignment, or are just reading in a café — it can crush our self-esteem and self-worth...
